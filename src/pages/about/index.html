A different kind of supplier.

Most countertop suppliers control one link of the chain and apologize for the rest. Judestone was built the other way around - as a supply platform that owns the chain end to end, so a builder's schedule never depends on someone else's.

01 · The promise

Construction Execution Certainty™

Every builder carries risk they can't see: the material that slips, the container nobody can trace, the spec change that quietly resets the clock. Judestone exists to take that risk from them. Because we own every link from quarry to jobsite, the schedule answers to one standard - and to one accountable partner.

One owner. One standard. Nobody left to blame.

02 · Why the platform exists

Countertops are a small line item with an outsized ability to stall a building.

A distributor places your order and waits. An importer blames the fabricator, the fabricator blames freight, and the schedule slips while everyone is technically doing their job. Judestone exists because that structure cannot be fixed from inside it - only replaced. We took ownership of every link so there is nobody left to wait on.

Who we build for

Multifamily general contractors and national builders - the teams carrying a schedule across hundreds of units, several buildings, and often several markets at once.

How we're measured

Not on the price of a slab. On whether the material landed when the building was ready for it, and whether unit 400 matches the one your buyers toured.

03 · What we supply

Three material lanes, one program.

We don't push a material. Quartz, granite, and silica-free are each sourced, priced, and ready, so a change in spec is a change of lane rather than a new sourcing cycle. Cut-to-size and full-slab programs run across all three.
