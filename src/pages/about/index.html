A different kind of supplier.

Judestone is the strategic supply platform for multifamily construction. We don't sell countertops - we remove the risk they carry.

01 · The platform

Judestone is not a countertop supplier. Judestone owns the supply.

Direct sourcing. Strategic manufacturing partnerships. Containers moving on our schedule - which means on yours. There is no middleman to wait on, because there is no middleman. When something matters, we never have to say "we're waiting on our supplier." We are the supplier.

Sourced.

Quarry and factory partnerships across the globe - qualified, audited, and held to Enterprise standards.

Streamlined.

Shipping strategies engineered to compress delivery - consolidated containers, priority routings, and direct-to-market deployment from the moment your PO lands.

Shipped.

Container logistics and import compliance handled in-house, sequenced to your construction schedule.

02 · What we supply

Materials are tools. Strategy is the product.

We match the material to your budget, your schedule, your owner, and your market - then stand behind it. Quartz, granite, and silica-free, with factory cut-to-size, slab programs, and dedicated factory capacity across all three categories.

01

Quartz

Consistent color and pattern at scale, engineered performance, and program pricing across thousands of units. View the quartz designs.

02

Granite

Proven in the field for decades, globally available, and sourced direct from quarry partners we know by name.

03

Silica-free

A fully sourced, fully spec'd lane for projects where regulation or owner requirements call for engineered alternatives.

04

Cut-to-size & sinks

Factory-finished pieces that arrive ready to set, and undermount sinks spec'd alongside the quartz so one supplier covers the whole top. Browse sink models.
