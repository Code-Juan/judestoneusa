Built for Volume

One kitchen or four hundred units. Same finish, same schedule.

Judestone serves projects of all sizes, and is built around bulk procurement and delivery for multifamily and large-scale construction.

  • Project-level pricing

    Quotes priced across five material groups for the full unit count - not slab by slab - so budgeting a building is as simple as budgeting a kitchen.

  • Consistency at scale

    Engineered quartz keeps pattern and color consistent from the model unit to the last building, so unit 400 matches the one your buyers toured.

  • Delivery that follows the build

    Bulk orders are staged and delivered for multifamily and large-scale builds, so material lands when the construction schedule needs it.

  • Samples before commitments

    Order samples, compare designs side by side, and save favorites - see the material in your own light before a project rides on it.
