04 · What to send

None of it is required to start a conversation. It is what turns a first call into a priced program.

01

Unit mix & count

How many units, of what types, across how many buildings - the basis for program pricing rather than slab-by-slab quoting.

02

Drawings or plans

When each building needs material, so deployment is sequenced per building, per floor, per phase.

Owner requirements, budget targets, and any regulatory constraints that point toward quartz, granite, or silica-free.
